Tile Trip: Thailand Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

This policy explains what information the Tile Trip: Thailand game ("the app") handles. The app is published by Tungthong Apps ("we", "us").

Your saved game

Your game progress (levels, stars, best times, Express Train clears, Travel Coins, power-ups, Travel Journal stamps and postcards, Daily Postcard stamps and achievements) and your settings (such as volume, haptics and language) are saved on your device. We don't have servers, and we never receive this information.

Android backup. If backup is turned on for your device, Android may copy your saved game to a private folder in your Google account, so it can come back when you install the app again or set up a new phone. This is a feature of Android, run by Google and protected by your Google account. You can turn it off in your device's backup settings (the menu names vary by device).

Deleting your saved game. Settings → Reset progress in the app erases your progress and keeps your settings, and uninstalling the app removes both from your device. If Android backup is on, a copy may stay in your Google account after you uninstall, and your progress may return if you install the app again. Turn off backup in your device's settings if you don't want that.

The app has no accounts, no sign-in and no chat, and it doesn't ask for your name, email, photos, contacts or location.

Advertising

The app shows optional rewarded video ads through Google AdMob. You choose whether to watch one, for example to keep going after losing a level or to get a free power-up. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers
  • approximate location (from your IP address)
  • how you interact with ads, and app diagnostics such as crash logs

Google uses this information as described in its policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Your choices

  • If you're in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options.
  • If you live in a US state with a consumer privacy law (for example California), you can opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of your personal information for targeted advertising in Settings → Privacy options.
  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, under Settings → Privacy → Ads on your device (the menu names vary by device).

Your rights in the EEA, UK and Switzerland (GDPR)

For the purposes of the GDPR and UK GDPR, Tungthong Apps is the controller of personal data processed through the app, and you can reach us at support@tungthongapps.com. Google acts as an independent controller for the advertising data it collects.

Legal basis. We rely on your consent to show personalised ads and to store or read identifiers on your device for advertising. If you don't consent, Google may still show non-personalised ads, which it may do on the basis of legitimate interests as described in Google's policies.

Your rights. You have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability, and to withdraw your consent at any time in Settings → Privacy options (this doesn't affect processing that happened before you withdrew). You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

We don't keep any personal data on our own servers, so most requests about advertising data are handled by Google. We don't hold a copy of your saved game either, so you manage it yourself, as described in Your saved game. Email us and we'll help where we can.

Your rights in the United States

This section applies to residents of US states with consumer privacy laws, such as the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the CPRA.

Information collected. Through the Google Mobile Ads SDK, the following categories of personal information may be collected: identifiers (such as your advertising ID and IP address), internet or other electronic network activity (such as ad interactions and app diagnostics), and approximate geolocation.

Sale and sharing. We don't sell personal information for money. However, making this information available to Google for targeted advertising may count as a "sale" or "sharing" of personal information, or as "targeted advertising", under some state laws.

Your rights. Depending on where you live, you may have the right to know what personal information is collected about you, to access, correct or delete it, and to opt out of its sale, sharing or use for targeted advertising. You can opt out at any time in Settings → Privacy options. We don't hold a copy of your saved game; to delete it, see Your saved game. For other requests, email support@tungthongapps.com. We won't discriminate against you for using any of these rights.

Data retention

Game progress stays on your device until you reset it or uninstall the app, and settings until you uninstall the app. In an Android backup, Google keeps only the latest copy and deletes backups that have gone unused for a while. Google keeps advertising data according to its own retention policies.

Children

The app is intended for people aged 13 and over and isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children.
